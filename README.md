I work under Scrum, coordinate with both technical and business teams, and use AI tools like **Claude Code and Codex** in my daily workflow, always reviewing and validating the generated code.
 
While I enjoy working across the whole stack, I have a special love for **Frontend Development and UI/UX Design**, _guided by the belief that a good interface shouldn't raise questions_.
 
In addition to my commitment to technological development, I'm also an enthusiast of cats, video games, coffee, and Studio Ghibli movies.
